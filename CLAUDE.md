## Working with the user

- The user writes in European Portuguese. Reply in it.
- Before building a section, raise every doubt and every change you would make (copy fixes, language, layout reading, photo quality) in one batch, with a recommended option first and concrete previews. Then build the finished section, not a prototype.
- Read free-text answers closely: they may rewrite the brief rather than pick an option. When the user delegates a choice, decide and say what you chose.

## The opening

- It is fired by scroll, not driven by it. The first scroll gesture (or a left click on the black) is a one-shot trigger; the run owns its own clock from start to finish, the page is locked for its duration and handed back with the second screen already on it. Do not reintroduce scrub.
- Drive everything from one clock MotionValue that `animate()` runs, not from `useScroll`.
- Outgoing and incoming states must overlap. No frame may be left with nothing on screen.
- One continuous move beats an effect: reach for a single deliberate gesture before particles or clever behaviour.

## Copy and imagery

- Copy the user gives in Portuguese keeps its content exactly; only the writing is adapted so it reads naturally in English, the way the user adapts "more for your money" to "e mais dinheiro para ti" in Portuguese. Never change what it says, never translate word for word (user, 2026-10-16).
- Portuguese copy never addresses the reader: no "tu", no "você" or "o senhor". Recast the sentence instead (impersonal, the studio's side, "o cliente", buttons in the infinitive), meaning unchanged (user, 2026-10-18; see `docs/language-decisions.md`). The studio's emails to clients are the exception: they address the reader formally ("consigo", "esteja à vontade"), as the studio wrote them (user, 2026-10-29).
- Fix the studio's copy (grammar, spelling) but keep its voice. Shorten only if every piece of information survives; otherwise keep the information and accept the length. Never cut a clause to make a layout or animation fit; make the layout fit the copy.
- Photos are in colour and never generic stock (no handshake, laptop or lightbulb clichés). Prefer close-up, single-subject, metaphorical shots with a strong colour.
- Present image candidates (source, photographer, size) before downloading anything. Unsplash results include paid Unsplash+ images; only use ones whose page says free.

## Do not break

- Old, unused section components stay in `src/components/`. Ask before removing any of them.
- The bar's links are Home / Portfolio / About nevima / Services, and its one button is "Get your free proposal", which opens the free-proposal form (user, 2026-10-11; Side by side added on 2026-11-11 and turned into the Services menu on 2026-11-16). Services is a menu, not a link: it drops a panel under the bar with the side-by-side page and every service that has a page of its own, read from `SERVICES` so it cannot drift (`servicesMenu()` in `floating-nav.tsx`). Under the fold the same list is a second block inside the "Menu" panel. Portfolio is its own page (`/portfolio`, `portfolio.tsx`), as is Side by side (`/side-by-side`, `side-by-side.tsx`). Under 768px the links fold into "Menu" (user, 2026-10-19 at 640px; moved to 768px on 2026-11-11, because four links and the Portuguese labels no longer fit a small tablet beside the proposal). The numbers on the links are kept for 1024px and up, for the same reason: a black panel covering the whole screen under the bar (user, 2026-10-20), in the footer's style, with Home, Portfolio, About nevima and "Get a quote", and the Services list under them / "Pedir orçamento" (to `#work-together`) on the footer's moving white light with the wordmark across the foot (the bar's mark fades out while it is open, user 2026-10-20), "Get a quote" white with a gleam and the green light on its right; the proposal button is not on the bar under 768px (user, 2026-10-20, at the fold): there the bar is the wordmark and "Menu".
- Every page ends with `WorkTogether` ("Let’s work together." and the proposal form) right before `<Footer />`. A new page gets it too. The one exception is the portfolio (user, 2026-10-12): a single screen with no bar, no `WorkTogether` and no footer, only "Exit portfolio" and "Let's work together" (which opens the proposal form).
- The home page's opening plays on every fresh load of the home page, reloads included (user, 2026-10-13); a return to it from inside the site (the router, with no reload) skips it. The browser decides: `booted` (`src/lib/opened.ts`, set by `ReturnKeeper` once the site has woken up) plus the tab's `nevima:opened` flag (`src/lib/return-spot.ts`). No cookie, no server decision.
- "Exit portfolio" goes back exactly where the visitor was (`src/lib/return-spot.ts`, `ReturnKeeper` in the layout; the home page skips its opening when it is returned to). Links into the portfolio must stay plain links to `/portfolio` so the spot is noted.
- Links between pages are plain `<a href>`; `PageWipe` (layout, `src/components/page-wipe.tsx`) follows them with the router, instantly and with no transition (the growing page wipe was removed, user 2026-10-13: do not bring it back). "Exit portfolio" leaves through `fadeThen` / `goTo`.
- Lenis owns the scroll lock through `useSmoothScroll(locked)` in `src/components/smooth-scroll.ts`. Do not move `stop()`/`start()` back into callers.
- Any Motion `layout` element below the opening must carry `layoutDependency`, or the jump when the opening releases the page gets animated.
- No numbers on titles. No boomerang pass on CTAs. No inner highlight inside the drop-shaped CTAs. Pixel looks are modern, never retro/CRT.
