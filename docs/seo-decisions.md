# SEO decision log

What was decided for the site's SEO and GEO (search engines and AI assistants), and why. Newest notes sit lower; when two notes disagree, the later one wins.

The base is the SEO audit of a previous project (Bsmartish, `Auditoria-SEO-BSMARTISH.pdf`, pp. 5–8 and 21–22): its 34 practices taken as principles, and its 15 gaps taken as mistakes not to repeat here. The work is in phases, so nothing is built twice while the copy is still changing:

- **Phase A, structure** (done 2026-11-03): everything that does not change with the copy. New pages and new words flow into it on their own.
- **Phase B, finishing** (when the copy is final): titles and descriptions written for search, headings, alt text, images through `next/image`, a speed measurement, `AboutPage` data, `llms.txt`, opening the about and portfolio pages to search engines.
- **Phase C, launch** (when the domain is linked): nevima.studio pointed at Vercel; www, nevima.pt and the `*.vercel.app` address sent to it with a 308; Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools verified and given the sitemap; a Google Business Profile as a service-area business once the address question is settled; links from clients' sites ("Website by nevima").
- **Phase D, growth** (after launch): pages for searches that do not name the brand (one per kind of client the brief names: clinics, estate agents), the web design page, each project at its own address.

## 2026-11-03, phase A

Decisions the user took (asked in one batch, recommended option first):

1. **All crawlers are let in**, AI search and AI training ones alike (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended included): a small studio gains more from the models knowing it than from keeping its words out of them. `src/app/robots.ts` has one rule for all (`*`), closing only `/api/`; a named group would replace that rule for its crawler.
2. **Each language declares itself from the server.** The single root layout wrote `<html lang="en">` on Portuguese pages too (a script corrected it in the browser): the Bsmartish mistake L15. Bing reads the attribute, and Bing feeds ChatGPT search and Copilot. Now `src/app/(en)/layout.tsx` and `src/app/pt/layout.tsx` are both root layouts around one document (`src/components/site-root.tsx`). Cost: switching language loads the page anew; the foot's switch already keeps the visitor's place, and the home page does not play its opening for a visitor who only changed language (`switchingTo` in `return-spot.ts`). `html-lang.tsx` and `LANG_SCRIPT` were removed.
3. **Portuguese addresses in Portuguese** (`/pt/sobre`, `/pt/servicos/identidade-visual`…): no SEO downside, the words show in results and shared links, and it had to be decided before launch, since a live address never changes without a redirect. No accents. One table, `PT_PATHS` in `src/lib/i18n.ts` (see `docs/language-decisions.md`).
4. **Vercel Speed Insights**: page speed as visitors feel it (Core Web Vitals), no cookies. The component is in the document; it measures only once it is switched on in the Vercel project (Speed Insights tab). Before switching it on, the privacy policy gets a line saying so, in both languages: the policy says the site uses no analytics tools. Until it is on, the browser logs a 404 for its script.
5. **No profiles elsewhere yet**: the organisation's `sameAs` is empty and ready. When the studio has profiles they go in both the footer and `SAME_AS` in `src/lib/seo.ts`, the same ones (Bsmartish mistake L13).
6. **The image a shared link shows** is drawn for each page and language at build time (`src/app/og/[lang]/[...page]/route.tsx`): black, the wordmark small at the top, the page's name large at the foot in Satoshi Medium. It follows the page's words on its own. Satoshi is fetched from Fontshare's API as TrueType at build time: the image renderer cannot read the site's WOFF2, and the font's licence forbids converting it. If Fontshare is down the image is drawn in the renderer's face rather than failing the build.

What was built:

- **One list of every page search engines may see**, `src/lib/seo.ts` (`SEO_PAGES`): its address, whether it may be listed (`index`), its title and description in both languages, what a shared link shows. Each page's metadata (`pageMetadata`), the sitemap, the share image and the structured data come from it, so a page can never be in the sitemap and closed to search engines at once. Opening the about page or the portfolio is one word. A new page gets its line there. The words are the pages' existing ones, moved, not rewritten (that is phase B); descriptions longer than 160 characters (the services') are cut at the last whole word for search results only.
- **Sitemap** (`src/app/sitemap.ts`): the listed pages in both languages, each with its other language and `x-default`. No `lastmod`: there is no record of when each page changed, and a made-up date (the build's) teaches search engines to ignore them (Bsmartish L12).
- **Closed off nevima.studio**: every response from any other host carries `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` (`next.config.ts`), so the Vercel address is never indexed as a copy of the site. It lifts itself once the site is served from nevima.studio.
- **Structured data** (JSON-LD, `src/components/json-ld.tsx`): on every page the studio as an `Organization` (name, logo, email, languages, founders), its two founders as `Person` (name and role only; the bios are still stand-ins) and the `WebSite`; on each service page a `Service` and its `BreadcrumbList`. Not a `LocalBusiness`: there is no public address. No `FAQPage`: Google stopped showing FAQ results on 2026-05-07.
- **Icons**: `apple-icon` (180px) and `logo.png` (512px, the organisation's logo; search engines ask for at least 112px) drawn from `icon.svg`.
- **A page for addresses the site does not have** (`src/app/global-not-found.tsx`, Next's `experimental.globalNotFound`; with two root layouts a 404 inside either is rendered as an empty error shell). It answers 404 and is noindex; drawn once for both languages, it follows the address in the browser (`not-found-switch.tsx`), so a Portuguese address never shows English first. Every page there is, the site's own and the services, and like every page it ends on "Let's work together" and the foot.
- **Security headers** (`next.config.ts`): HSTS (no `includeSubDomains` or preload until the domain's subdomains are known), `nosniff`, a referrer policy, no camera, microphone or location.
- **SEO check** (`audit/seo-check.mjs`): run against a production build, it reads every page in the sitemap and every page they link to as search engines receive it, and fails on: wrong `<html lang>`, missing or duplicate titles, a missing description or canonical, hreflang missing or not returned, not exactly one `<h1>`, an image without `alt`, invalid structured data, a missing share image, a link to a page that is not there (an untranslated Portuguese address), a sitemap page closed to search engines, a missing page that does not answer 404. It warns on long descriptions and on Portuguese pages whose alt text is the same as the English page's (Bsmartish L7). Run it after each round of copy changes.

Already right before phase A, by the way the site was built: pages rendered on the server with their text, the Portuguese under its own addresses, canonical and hreflang on every page (`alternates()` in `i18n.ts`), the language switch a real link (Bsmartish L11), the FAQ's answers in the HTML even when closed, stand-in pages closed to search engines, fonts self-hosted with `swap`.
