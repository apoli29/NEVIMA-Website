- 2026-10-11, motion and UX audit (user asked for a real pass with the design-motion-principles and ui-ux-pro-max skills; the user delegated the weighting: "muito limpo e minimalista", so Jakub (polish) first, Emil (restraint) second, Jhey selective). Fixed:
  - The large card no longer animates width or height (a layout property every frame). On a computer the photo column is cut to the card's own 3:4 (`--fo-photo` = height × 0.75), so the card is flown into it by transform only (translate, even scale, turn; 0.6s, ease-out); then the large card's paper and edge (`.fo-frame`) open out from behind it with clip-path (0.45s) and the words come in (0.3s, 8px). Leaving is quicker and quieter: words out 0.12s, frame back 0.3s, card home 0.45s on an ease-in curve. On a phone the sheet simply rises 16px into place (0.4s) and drops 8px away (0.25s). The card is drawn at the hand's pixels from the first frame, so it is never seen anywhere else.
  - The caption under the hand no longer snaps when the name changes: 200ms fade with a 3px rise. Photos fade in when they arrive (0.4s) instead of popping over the name.
  - On touch screens, where nothing can be pointed at, each card carries its name on the black marker (`.fo-card-tag`, `(hover: none)` only).
  - Close is a 44px-tall target. Small informational text in the portfolio (caption status, "Full report coming soon", photo credit) is in `--color-ash` for contrast; field labels keep the site's `ash-2`.
  - The darkening of the card left behind uses the site's ease-out curve, not the default `ease`.
  Left as is, on purpose: the gleam over "See our work." and the index ask (user: hard to miss), with reduced motion respected.
